🏛️ The Legacy of KPR Hotel

Established in 1998 in Madurai, KPR Hotel has been serving authentic South Indian Tamil Nadu cuisine prepared with freshly ground spices, cold-pressed sesame oil, and traditional woodfire cooking techniques.

🌾 Pure Seeraga Samba Rice

Our Biryanis use authentic aromatic Seeraga Samba rice sourced directly from farmers in Tanjore.

🔥 Woodfire Cooking

Slow cooked in heavy brass degs over wood fires for signature smoky flavor and tenderness.

🍃 Fresh Banana Leaf Service

Traditional eco-friendly banana leaf meals served with unlimited sambar, rasam, and kootu.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How does the AI Voice ordering agent work?

Press the microphone button and speak in English, Tamil, or Tanglish (transliterated Tamil like "Enaku 2 biryani venum"). The AI detects your language, parses your order, and adds it to your live cart.

What payment methods are supported?

We support instant UPI QR codes (scannable via GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), Cash on Delivery, and Credit/Debit cards.

What are your operating hours?

Open daily from 7:00 AM to 11:00 PM for breakfast, lunch, dinner, and late-night snacks.
